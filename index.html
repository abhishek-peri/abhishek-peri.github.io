Abhishek Peri

Senior ML Engineer · Qualcomm Technologies

I'm a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Qualcomm in San Diego, where I work on end-to-end autonomous driving research, including vision-language-action (VLA) models and world-action models (WAMs), with a focus on bringing these systems to embedded automotive hardware.

I hold an M.S. in Intelligent Systems, Robotics & Control from UC San Diego, where I worked in the Statistical Visual Computing Laboratory under Prof. Nuno Vasconcelos, and a B.Tech in Electrical Engineering from IIT Madras. Previously, I built smartphone-to-cloud 3D reconstruction and Gaussian Splatting systems at OpenCV.org with Gary Bradski, semantic SLAM and 3DGS for XR at Magic Leap, and production computer vision for AR virtual try-on.

My research spans rotation-equivariant local features, controllable diffusion models (NeurIPS 2024), and end-to-end autonomous driving with vision-language-action and world-action models. I'm also a reviewer for NeurIPS.
